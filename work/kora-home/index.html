How do you fill a new store on opening weekend?

Outdoor, radio and digital media for the opening of a 6,000 m² furniture flagship.

Client
Kora Home
Industry
Retail
Services
Campaigns & media
Strategy & consulting
Location
Doha, Qatar
Identity

The challenge

Kora Home was opening its first flagship, a 6,000 m² furniture store on the edge of the city. Nobody walks past it. The store needed traffic on the opening weekend and a steady flow after, with eight weeks to plan and buy the media.

The solution

We planned the buy around the drive. Billboards on the three routes into the store counted down the kilometres. Radio ran during the evening commute. Digital ads targeted people who had just bought or rented a home. One promise ran through everything: furnish a room in one visit.

The project included:

  • a media plan across outdoor, radio and digital;
  • negotiation and booking of 38 outdoor sites and 3 radio stations;
  • a route-based billboard sequence;
  • geo-targeted digital ads for new movers;
  • footfall and sales tracking through opening month.

The results

  • 18,400 visitors in the first three days
  • opening weekend sales 2.3x the forecast
  • media cost per visitor 34% below plan
