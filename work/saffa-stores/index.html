Saffa Stores

  • Audit and assessment
  • Lahore, Pakistan
  • Strategy & consulting

How do you audit 42 stores that each tell a different story?

An end-to-end communication audit for a grocery chain that had grown faster than its voice.

Client
Saffa Stores
Industry
Retail
Services
Strategy & consulting
Brand & creative
Location
Lahore, Pakistan
Identity

The challenge

Saffa Stores opened 42 neighbourhood grocery stores in nine years. Each manager had printed their own signs, run their own WhatsApp groups and boosted their own Facebook posts. The result: four logos in use, eleven different price-tag designs and a loyalty card most customers did not know existed. Leadership wanted to know what to fix first, and what it was costing them.

The solution

We visited 18 stores in person, photographed every touchpoint and catalogued 1,140 assets. We interviewed 64 shoppers at the till and read two years of social and sales data. The audit compared Saffa with three competitors on the same streets.

The project included:

  • a touchpoint census of 1,140 assets across 18 stores;
  • shopper interviews and a competitor walk-through;
  • a 46-page diagnosis written in plain Urdu and English;
  • a ranked action plan: 23 fixes, each with cost and owner;
  • a one-page signage and price-tag standard, rolled out first.

The results

  • the first five fixes were live in all 42 stores within eight weeks
  • loyalty card sign-ups rose 3.4x in the following quarter
  • print spend fell 28% once every store used one shared template set
