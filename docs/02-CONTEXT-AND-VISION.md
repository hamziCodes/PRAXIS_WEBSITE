# Praxis Studio — Context & Vision
*The full background for a Claude instance starting cold. Read this first, then the Brand Book PDF, then the Prompt. Everything here is real context gathered over weeks of work — trust it, and build on it.*

## Who we are
**Praxis Studio** is a new, design-first studio based in Islamabad, Pakistan, working with clients worldwide. Tagline: **"Theory made real."**

Three people, no titles, ever — on the website they appear as names only:
**HAMZA · LAIBA · SHAHZAIB** — internally nicknamed "the trifecta." Laiba is the designer (she owns the final logo files); Hamza and Shahzaib run strategy, web and growth between them. The studio is one umbrella: strategy, branding, creative, web/digital, campaigns, SEO/AEO and marketing.

Praxis is a fresh start, deliberately decoupled from any other company the founders run. Do not reference, link, or borrow content from any other brand or product of theirs — if it is not in this package, it does not exist for this website.

## Why this website, why now
The studio is launching. Real client work exists but is under NDA and cannot be shown — so the website launches with **generated concept projects** (see 04-WORK-GENERATION.md) that demonstrate the studio's design language at full quality, until the designers' own demo projects and cleared client work swap in. Every concept project carries a small, honest note saying so.

## The model: Bitwip
The structure and design anatomy come from **bitwip.fr** (French communications agency), chosen by the founder personally. The instruction is a faithful rebuild — same sitemap, same page templates, same section order, same motion quality — re-skinned completely in the Praxis brand and rewritten in Praxis words. Bitwip's own text, orange, photos and French-overseas content never carry over. The founder can supply a screen recording of Bitwip if motion detail is needed.

Why Bitwip fits: light cream editorial pages with black anchor panels (matches our Bone-based light direction), one asterisk motif repeated everywhere (mirrors our star-x), one display word inside big grotesk headlines, an accordion for services with a colour per service, case studies built around the client's *question*, and a giant wordmark footer.

## The look — locked decisions (do not relitigate)
- **LIGHT, warm-neutral, Bone-led.** An earlier internal document said "dark-first" — that was an error and is revoked. Dark appears only as contained moments: the services panel, project galleries, the closing/footer. Heroes are light.
- **Colours (exact hex in the Brand Book):** Bone background, Black text, Inchworm as the single hero accent, Gunmetal for galleries/closing, Orange + Pale Violet as rare sparks inside pixel graphics and work imagery only. Maximum two accents on screen.
- **Type:** Helvetica Neue for everything main. **Handjet** (free, Google Fonts, file included in assets) for small pixel labels/tags only — never body copy. A serif appears as **one display word** inside a big headline, nowhere else (which serif is an open choice — see below).
- **Logo (decided):** the mark is a **pixel starburst (centre square, four diagonal squares, 8 satellite squares - plates in the Brand Book)** (Option 3 in the references) + the wordmark from Option 4, where **the x in "Praxis" is a chunky 4-petal star** at full letter height and weight. Recreate the logo from the Brand Book plates as a finished production SVG, best of your ability — do not wait on anyone. Keep it as one reusable component so it can be swapped in one place if official exports ever replace it. Two earlier attempts failed in instructive ways: corner-squares around a centre (reads as dice/QR) and a tiny sparkle star (vanishes, reads "Pra—is"). The Brand Book draws the correct geometry.
- **Signatures:** a "pixel snake" — a trail of pixels dragging word pills (BRANDING / UIUX / MOTION / SEO) across the hero; images that assemble from chunky pixels on scroll-in and a headline that breaks back into pixels on scroll-away. One or two such moments, kept tasteful. The studio's name resonates with "pixel" — the pixel is the brand's graphic soul.
- **People:** "The people behind the work" — giant names only. No job titles, no designations, anywhere.
- **Services = four**, mirroring Bitwip's set (we genuinely offer the same): Strategy & consulting / Brand & creative / Web & digital / Campaigns & media — each with its own star colour, renamed and rewritten in our voice.
- **Honest numbers only:** the studio is new. Stats are small and true: 3 people / 6 services / 1 studio. No invented project counts, no fake client logos, no fake testimonials outside the clearly-labelled concept projects.

## How to work (the founder's rules)
- **You have the final say.** Nothing in this package is the final word on creative decisions. Research current best practice, and where you see a better way that still honours the vision above, take it — and say what you changed and why.
- **Unsure?** If a missing detail is cheap to decide, decide it in line with the vision and note it. If it genuinely changes direction, ask the user — briefly, one question at a time.
- **One company, one voice.** Direct, confident, no corporate filler. The site should feel like the best small studio in the room: calm, exact, a little playful with its pixels.
- **Quality bar:** this is the studio's own shop window. If the design or motion looks like a template, it has failed. Bitwip-level polish is the floor, not the ceiling.
