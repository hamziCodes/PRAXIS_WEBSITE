BUILD — the Praxis Studio website. Complete build, not a mockup.

You are starting cold, so everything you need is in this repo
under docs/:

  questions: the PDF wins.
- CONTEXT & VISION (MD): who we are, why this site, what is locked,
  what is open. Read it fully before building.
- WORK GENERATION (MD): the 16 portfolio projects to create, and the
  case-page anatomy.
- ASSETS: our original brand guidelines PDF, logo/colour/type/

- REFERENCE SITE: bitwip.fr — study the live site yourself;
  nobody needs to send you anything.

THE JOB
Rebuild Bitwip as our site: same sitemap, same page templates, same
section anatomy, same motion quality — re-skinned entirely in the
Praxis brand and rewritten in our words. Faithful rebuild, not
"inspired by". Their text, orange, photos and content never carry
over. Pages: Home / Services index / 4 service pages / Work index /
16 project pages (one template) / About / Journal index + article
template (3 complete articles) / Contact / Glossary (the full
A-Z glossary, ~45 terms in our voice, like the reference's) /
footer system. Build EVERYTHING — nothing is phase 2, nothing
is skipped, nothing is a placeholder.

BRAND, IN SHORT (detail in the Brand Book)
Light, Bone-led (#DFE0D0), black text, Inchworm (#B1FA63) the one
hero accent; Gunmetal only for galleries/closing; Orange + Pale
Violet as rare sparks inside pixel graphics and work images. Max 2
accents per screen. Helvetica Neue everywhere main; Handjet (file
attached) for pixel labels/tags only; a serif for ONE display word
in big headlines only — choose the serif, it is your call. Logo: pixel-X blob mark
with its 8 satellite squares (Brand Book plates) + wordmark whose x is a chunky 4-petal star at
full x weight (geometry plated in the Brand Book. RECREATE the
logo from those plates as a finished, production-quality SVG —
best of your ability, do not wait for designer files. Build it as
one reusable SVG symbol so it can be swapped in one place if the
founders later supply official exports). Signature moments: pixel snake dragging word pills in
the hero; images assembling from pixels on scroll-in. People shown
as giant names only — HAMZA / LAIBA / SHAHZAIB, "the people behind
the work", no titles anywhere. Stats consistent with the site:
3 people / 16 projects / 4 service areas. Live Islamabad clock + OPEN status in the
hero, like the reference's office clocks.

WORK
Generate the 16 portfolio projects exactly per the Work Generation
file: 4 per category, each a different subcategory, full case pages
(question headline, meta, challenge/solution/results, one metric
where it fits, 7–10 generated gallery images per project in our art
direction). KOFFA, LEDGERLY and VELOUR hold their slots. Every
project gets a derived client company name, its own logo and
brand colours, and one client testimonial (named person + role).
The testimonials slider and the client logo wall are part of the
build, like the reference's. NO caveats, labels or disclaimers
anywhere — the portfolio is presented as the studio's work.
The generated set is the site's portfolio — complete and final.
Build projects as self-contained data blocks so the founders can
edit or replace one easily later, by their own choice — explain
the block structure in your delivery notes. Contact email
everywhere: hello@praxis.studio.

MOTION & QUALITY
This is a design studio's own shop window — Bitwip-level polish is
the floor. Smooth scroll, real drag carousel, accordion physics,
scroll reveals, the pixel moments. But robustness is part of
quality: if any library CDN fails, the content must still appear
and any preloader must dismiss itself — a visitor must never be
stuck on a loading screen. Mobile is a first-class citizen: the
header pill must never overflow or wrap at 390px.

DECISION RIGHTS
You have the final say. Nothing we gave you is the last word:
research best practice, and where you find a better way that honours
the vision, take it and note what you changed and why. If a missing
detail is cheap to decide, decide it in line with the vision and
note it. Never ask, never wait: if anything is missing or
undecided, decide it yourself in line with the vision and deliver
complete — the founders will change what they don't like. Do not invent facts about the studio beyond what the Context
file gives you about the FOUNDERS (three named people, Islamabad,
a new studio — invent no different founders, city or founding
story). The portfolio world itself — clients, logos, testimonials,
results — is yours to create in full.

DELIVER
The complete website, ready to host, committed to the repo
github.com/hamziCodes/PRAXIS_WEBSITE (site files in the repo root;
this prereq package lives in its docs/ folder — never overwrite
docs/), plus brief delivery notes:
structure, how a project block is edited or replaced, how the logo component is built,
and every decision you made for us. Build it like our clients are
watching — they are.
